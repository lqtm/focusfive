# Focus Five privacy policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

Focus Five is a local-first Chrome extension that replaces the new tab page with a five-item focus list. It does not collect, sell, transmit, or analyze personal data.

## Data stored on your device

Focus Five stores your tasks, ideas, hints, routines, categories, Daybook history and daily note, timer settings, theme and presentation preferences, and the websites you add to the blocklist in `chrome.storage.local`. This data stays in your browser profile until you delete it or uninstall the extension.

Focus Five has no account, backend, analytics, telemetry, advertising, or remote code. Fonts and wallpapers are bundled inside the extension.

## Backups

When you choose **Export backup** in Settings, Focus Five saves a JSON file of your data to your computer through the browser's normal download. When you choose **Restore from file**, it reads only the file you pick. Backup files never leave your device unless you move them yourself.

## Browser permissions

- `storage` saves your data locally.
- `alarms` keeps recurring tasks, the focus timer, and temporary blocklist pauses on schedule when no new tab is open.
- `declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess` lets Chrome send websites on your blocklist to the Focus Five page while you still have open work today.
- Optional site access is requested one website at a time, only when you add that website to your blocklist. Chrome asks you each time, and you can revoke it in the extension's site-access settings. Focus Five never reads page contents, cookies, or browsing history; Chrome applies the blocking rule without showing the extension the pages you visit.

Photographer credits in Presentation mode link to Unsplash and open only if you click them.

## Data sharing

Focus Five does not share data with its developer or anyone else. There is no server to receive it.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this repository and shipped with a new extension version. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/lqtm/focusfive/issues.
